# Product Sheet: Fern™ Task Chair

## Product Name
Fern™

## Description
The Fern task chair feels alive and in tune with your every movement. Grounded in extensive science-based ergonomic research, Fern's unique and innovative design focuses on the person and the holistic experience of sitting. The result is a groundbreaking sitting experience that is distinctively responsive to each individual. Fern works with you, not against you so you can sit better, work better and feel better in today's changing workspace.

## Overview
Fern's Wave Suspension™ system is the heart of the chair and the key to its back comfort and flexibility. The back may look simple from outside, but inside it has a high level of science, engineering, and innovation. The design is inspired by a fern plant:
- **Stem™**: A centered spine that supports a series of Fronds.
- **Fronds™**: Calibrated to support different areas of the back from thoracic to lumbar to pelvic.
- **Cradle™**: Overlays the Fronds and Stem for effortless support, providing a soft and pliable Infinity Edge™ for edgeless comfort without interference during movement.
